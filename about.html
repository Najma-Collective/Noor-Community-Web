Our Story & Mission

Our Founding Conviction: Education as a Practice of Self-Determination.

We are an international network of educators united by a shared vision of liberation through education. We believe that in contexts of sustained adversity, the continuity of learning is a vital act of cultural and intellectual preservation—nurturing the conditions for a future of one's own making.

Our Story

Our work began in 2024 when two British Council teachers, Areej Banat and Gabriel Ishmael Beckford, established a small ELT unit serving students at Al Manar Society.

Driven by a desire to use their skills to support Palestinian communities, their small group of volunteers has since expanded into a global "community of practice," offering a range of programmes that work alongside Palestinians across the region and in the diaspora.

school Our Philosophy

A Pedagogy of Possibility

We see education as a practice of freedom. It is a means to expand the "substantive freedoms"—the capabilities—that enable individuals to lead lives they have reason to value.

Our curriculum is not about the passive transmission of knowledge. It is about the active cultivation of creativity and criticality, empowering students to solve problems, articulate their vision, and engage productively with the world.
