Your Next Step

01

Step 1: Initial Application

Complete and submit the online application form with your personal details, academic history, and programme preference.

02

Step 2: Placement Interview

If eligible, you will be invited to a 30-minute virtual interview with a programme coordinator to assess your goals and skill level for accurate placement.

03

Step 3: Programme Onboarding

Successful applicants will receive an acceptance notification and be given access to our online learning platform and all course materials.
