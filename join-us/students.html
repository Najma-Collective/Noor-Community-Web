Am I Eligible?

Check these requirements before applying. If you meet them, we want to hear from you!

✓ Eligibility Checklist

  • ✓ Refugee, Asylum Seeker, or Displaced Person Status
    You are currently displaced due to conflict, persecution, or crisis. Documentation may be requested but is not required to apply.
  • ✓ Minimum Age: 16 years old
    Our programmes are designed for adult learners. Youth under 18 require parental consent.
  • ✓ Basic English Proficiency (A2 level or above)
    You can understand simple conversations and read basic texts. Not sure? We'll help assess your level.
  • ✓ Reliable Internet Access
    Our classes are online via Zoom. You need a stable internet connection and a device (smartphone, tablet, or computer).
  • ✓ Commitment to Attend Classes
    Programmes run 5-10 hours/week for 12 weeks. We ask for 80% attendance. We know life as a displaced person is unpredictable—we're here to support you.

The Application Process

1

Submit Interest Form

Complete the simple form below (5 minutes). Tell us about yourself and why you want to learn English.

2

Level Assessment

We'll email you a short placement test and schedule a 15-minute conversation to assess your level and needs.

3

Get Accepted & Enroll

If accepted, you'll receive your programme placement, class schedule, and onboarding materials. Classes start every 8 weeks.
