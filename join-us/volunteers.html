Why Volunteer with Noor?

This isn't typical volunteer work. It's a global community of educators committed to justice, excellence, and solidarity.

🌍 Global Impact

Your teaching directly enables students to access university scholarships, professional employment, and civic participation. You're not just teaching English—you're unlocking futures.

🎓 Professional Development

Access our training in trauma-informed pedagogy, culturally responsive teaching, and online facilitation. Join a global network of expert educators. Grow your practice.

💚 Authentic Relationships

This isn't a one-way transaction. You'll build real relationships with your students and fellow volunteers. Many educators say their students have taught them as much as they've taught.

⏰ Flexible Commitment

Most roles require ~5-8 hours per week. Choose class times that fit your schedule. We understand you have a life—we'll work with you.

🛠️ Full Support

You'll receive comprehensive onboarding, curriculum materials, ongoing mentorship, and access to our volunteer community. You're never alone.

🏆 Excellence-Focused

We're not looking for "just volunteers." We're building a world-class teaching team. Your expertise matters, and we'll challenge you to bring your best.

What We Offer You

Before your question "What can I give?", we ask: What do you need to thrive?

  • ✓ Comprehensive Onboarding: 2-week training in Noor pedagogy, trauma-informed teaching, and platform tools
  • ✓ Ready-Made Curriculum: Detailed lesson plans, activities, and materials—you don't have to start from scratch
  • ✓ Ongoing Mentorship: Paired with an experienced Noor educator for your first semester
  • ✓ Global Community: Join our Slack workspace, monthly meetups, and annual volunteer summit
  • ✓ Professional Recognition: Verified letter of recommendation, LinkedIn endorsement, volunteer certificate
  • ✓ Growth Opportunities: Pathways to curriculum design, teacher training, and leadership roles

What We Ask of You

Qualifications & Requirements

📜 Teaching Credentials (One of the following):

  • ✓ CELTA, DELTA, or equivalent TESOL/TEFL certification
  • ✓ Master's degree in TESOL, Applied Linguistics, Education, or related field
  • ✓ Teaching license + 2+ years English teaching experience
  • ✓ Native/fluent English speaker + 3+ years teaching experience in any subject

Note: We evaluate applications holistically. If you don't meet these exactly but have relevant experience (e.g., tutoring, community education), please still apply and explain your background.

⏰ Time Commitment:

Most volunteer roles require ~5-8 hours per week for one semester (12 weeks):

  • ✓ 3-4 hours/week: Live class instruction
  • ✓ 2-3 hours/week: Prep, grading, student communication
  • ✓ 1 hour/week: Team meetings, professional development

We ask for a minimum one-semester commitment (12 weeks). Building trust with students requires consistency.

💻 Technical Requirements:

  • ✓ Reliable internet connection
  • ✓ Computer with webcam and microphone
  • ✓ Comfortable using Zoom, Google Classroom, and Slack

Volunteer Roles Available

Most Needed

English Instructor

Teach live, synchronous English classes (General, Business, or Academic English) via Zoom. Class sizes: 12-15 students.

Commitment: 5-8 hrs/week for 12 weeks
Qualifications: CELTA/DELTA or MA in TESOL

IELTS/TOEFL Specialist

Teach test preparation courses focused on IELTS or TOEFL exam strategies, practice, and feedback.

Commitment: 5-8 hrs/week for 12 weeks
Qualifications: IELTS/TOEFL teaching experience + certification

Writing Coach

Provide 1-on-1 feedback and coaching on academic or professional writing (essays, cover letters, personal statements).

Commitment: 3-5 hrs/week, flexible duration
Qualifications: Strong writing background + teaching experience

Conversation Partner

Lead informal conversation practice sessions (small groups or 1-on-1) to build students' speaking confidence.

Commitment: 2-4 hrs/week, flexible duration
Qualifications: Native/fluent English, patient communicator

Curriculum Developer

Design lesson plans, activities, and assessments for our programmes. Work collaboratively with our curriculum team.

Commitment: Project-based, flexible hours
Qualifications: Curriculum design experience + pedagogical expertise

Teacher Trainer

Facilitate onboarding and professional development workshops for new Noor volunteer educators.

Commitment: 5-10 hrs/month, ongoing
Qualifications: DELTA or MA + 5+ years teaching experience
