Partnerships

Our Network of Solidarity

Our work is made possible through collaboration with value-aligned partners who share our vision of education as a practice of freedom.

Featured Partner: Al Manar Society

We are proud to partner with Al Manar Society, where our work began in 2024. Al Manar Society has been instrumental in supporting Palestinian communities, and our collaboration continues to create meaningful educational opportunities for students across the region.

Our Supporters & Affiliates

Noor Community thrives because of a global solidarity network of schools, NGOs, and philanthropy partners who invest in healing-centered education for Palestinian youth.

From emergency education funds to regional grassroots organizations, each ally below strengthens a different part of our model—scholarships, mental-health informed pedagogy, technology access, and rapid-response microgrants. Together they allow our students to dream, plan, and build with confidence.

  • Education Cannot Wait · Emergency funding that keeps remote learning and crisis counseling open.

  • UNRWA · Coordination with UN schools to recognize Noor Community credentials.

  • Taawon · Grants for laptops, library resources, and mentoring stipends.

  • Anera · On-the-ground partnerships that extend psychosocial support to families.
