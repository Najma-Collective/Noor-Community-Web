Our Parent Organization

Al Manar Society

Noor Community is the English department of Al Manar Society, a pioneering organization dedicated to empowering Palestinian communities through education, culture, and social development.

account_balance About

Who They Are

Al Manar Society is a community-based organization committed to supporting Palestinian communities through comprehensive educational, cultural, and social programs. Founded on principles of empowerment and self-determination, they work to create opportunities for Palestinians to thrive despite challenging circumstances.

As our parent organization, Al Manar Society provides the foundation and infrastructure that makes Noor Community's English language programs possible.

What They Do

school

Educational Programs

Al Manar Society develops and delivers educational programs designed to empower Palestinian youth and adults with the knowledge and skills needed to pursue their goals and contribute to their communities.

diversity_3

Community Development

Through various social initiatives, Al Manar Society strengthens community bonds and creates supportive networks that help Palestinian families navigate challenges and access essential resources.

palette

Cultural Preservation

They champion Palestinian culture and heritage through programs that celebrate traditions, arts, and collective memory, ensuring that cultural identity remains strong across generations.

volunteer_activism

Advocacy & Support

Al Manar Society advocates for Palestinian rights and provides direct support to individuals and families, addressing immediate needs while working toward long-term systemic change.
