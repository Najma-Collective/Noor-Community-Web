Programme Overview

Find Your Pathway.

Our educational programmes are the practical application of our pedagogical vision. Each provides an academically rigorous and affirming experience, equipping you with the sophisticated linguistic and analytical tools to build your future.

Our English Language Programmes

We offer comprehensive English language education across multiple tracks, from general communication to academic preparation.

01

General & Business English

Master practical English for professional contexts through real-world problem-solving.

Our Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT) approach immerses you in authentic scenarios where you'll develop professional communication skills, business writing expertise, and workplace English fluency.

What You'll Learn:

  • check_circleProfessional email and report writing
  • check_circleBusiness presentations and negotiations
  • check_circleProject management communication
  • check_circleCV writing and interview preparation
  • check_circleStrategic planning and proposal development

Programme Details

Duration: 12-16 weeks per level

Levels: A2 (Pre-Intermediate) to C1 (Advanced)

Format: Online, live sessions

Schedule: 2-3 sessions per week, 90 minutes each

Class Size: 8-12 students maximum

Prerequisites: Basic English proficiency (A2+)

Featured Projects:

  • arrow_rightNGO Funding Challenge
  • arrow_rightCommunity Business Plan
  • arrow_rightProfessional Portfolio Development

02

English for Academic Purposes (EAP)

Prepare for university success with advanced academic English skills.

Based on the internationally recognized Oxford EAP curriculum, our programme develops the critical thinking, research, and academic writing skills essential for higher education.

What You'll Learn:

  • check_circleAcademic essay writing and argumentation
  • check_circleResearch skills and source evaluation
  • check_circleCritical reading and analysis
  • check_circleAcademic presentations and seminars
  • check_circleReferencing and citation (APA, MLA, Harvard)
  • check_circleNote-taking and lecture comprehension

Programme Details

Duration: 12-20 weeks per level

Levels: B1 (Intermediate) to C1 (Advanced)

Format: Online, live sessions + independent study

Schedule: 3 sessions per week, 90-120 minutes each

Class Size: 8-10 students maximum

Prerequisites: B1 level English or placement test

Assessment:

  • arrow_rightPortfolio-based continuous assessment
  • arrow_rightResearch project and dissertation
  • arrow_rightAcademic presentation skills evaluation
Full Programme Details arrow_forward
03

Skills Development & Exam Preparation

Targeted support for language exams and soft skills development.

Whether you're preparing for IELTS, TOEFL, or developing essential soft skills like leadership and communication, our specialized tracks provide focused, expert guidance.

Exam Preparation Tracks:

  • check_circleIELTS (Academic & General)
  • check_circleTOEFL iBT
  • check_circleCambridge Exams (FCE, CAE, CPE)
  • check_circleDuolingo English Test

Soft Skills Workshops:

  • check_circleLeadership and team management
  • check_circlePublic speaking and presentation
  • check_circleIntercultural communication
  • check_circleProfessional networking

Programme Details

Exam Prep Duration: 8-12 weeks

Format: 1-on-1 or small group (3-5 students)

Schedule: Flexible, customized to your needs

Focus: Test strategies, practice tests, skill building

Soft Skills Duration: 6-8 week workshops

Format: Interactive group sessions

Partnership: Delivered with ALanqa

Schedule: Weekly 2-hour workshops
