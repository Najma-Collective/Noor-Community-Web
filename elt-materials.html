Context and Pedagogical Vision

Your work will be grounded in the same ethos that guides our community: education as liberation, language as capability, and learning environments grounded in safety, choice, and deep respect for students' lived experience.

assignment Role Snapshot

Key Responsibilities

Work closely with our Academic Director and interdisciplinary team to evolve a structurally sound, responsive curriculum.

  • Author high-quality learning materials for a modular, online curriculum that pairs rigour with care.
  • Design task-based learning sequences that are linguistically effective and culturally sustaining.
  • Develop teacher support assets, from lesson plans to training guides, that keep pedagogy coherent.
  • Collaborate with software developers on interactive digital components that extend learner agency.
  • Audit and refine existing materials to align with our core principles and student feedback.
  • Incorporate insights from focus groups and classroom observations into each iteration.

workspace_premium Qualifications

Experience We Value

We welcome collaborators who are technically sharp, reflective practitioners committed to justice-driven education.

Essential

  • Proven track record writing engaging ELT materials for online, blended, or face-to-face delivery.
  • Deep expertise in Task-Based Learning and its practical application in materials design.
  • Experience integrating AI into ELT for content creation and personalised learning.
  • Substantial classroom ELT experience and comfort working remotely in a collaborative team.

Desirable

  • Experience working with students in crisis or low-resource contexts.
  • Background in curriculum design that promotes learner agency.
  • Experience developing materials across proficiency levels and for specific purposes.
