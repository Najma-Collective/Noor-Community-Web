Who We Are

Noor Community is an international network of educators and activists united by a conviction that education, particularly language education, functions as a primary practice of human liberation. We work alongside Palestinian grassroots organisations to provide high-level English language training, professional skills development, and academic mentoring to students from Gaza, the West Bank, and the diaspora.

Our work is grounded in the understanding that in contexts of sustained adversity, the classroom must function as a space of stability, possibility, and care. We recognise that intellectual rigour and emotional safety are mutually reinforcing, that the cognitive and affective dimensions of learning cannot be separated, particularly when students navigate continuous traumatic stress.

school Our Approach

Our Pedagogical Vision

We understand language proficiency as a dynamic capability that expands the substantive freedoms enabling individuals to lead lives they have reason to value. Our curriculum emphasises the cultivation of students as critical and creative agents, rather than passive recipients of knowledge, so they may use language to solve problems, articulate their vision with confidence, and engage productively with the challenges and opportunities before them.

The cultivation of individual agency is an inherently social process. Our classrooms are structured as horizontal, collaborative spaces where knowledge is co-constructed through dialogue and mutual inquiry—an engaged pedagogy of shared responsibility. From this foundation, we aim to create a space where our students' sense of local identity becomes the very basis for ethical and effective global engagement.

This entire framework rests upon an ethical commitment to student wellbeing. Our practice, grounded in solidarity and care, ensures that the classroom remains a space for intellectual exploration and personal growth, allowing students to build the confidence they need to thrive both academically and professionally.

1. Operations Coordinator

The Role

The Operations Coordinator serves as the logistical anchor for Noor Community during this phase of structural implementation. This role demands someone capable of managing the precise execution of recruitment, enrolment, and assessment protocols, maintaining the integrity of our day-to-day procedures while leveraging automation to ensure the organisation remains agile and responsive to the needs of our community.

Core Responsibilities

  • Oversee end-to-end administration of the recruitment lifecycle: candidate screening, reference verification, and availability mapping
  • Manage the integration of new educators and students into the Noor ecosystem, ensuring all participants are equipped with necessary resources and methodological briefings
  • Monitor bi-weekly assessment cadence and coordinate quarterly certification processes
  • Utilise AI-driven tools and prompt engineering to synthesise administrative data, draft communications, and optimise workflows
  • Maintain accuracy of the central database, ensuring applicant data flows seamlessly into scheduling and resource allocation systems

Who You Are

You have demonstrated experience in operational coordination or administrative management. You are proficient with AI-assisted productivity tools and comfortable working within the Google Workspace ecosystem. You bring strong organisational capabilities with attention to procedural clarity, and you understand that reliable systems are themselves a form of care for the communities they serve.

2. Systems Architect (Google Ecosystem)

The Role

We seek a developer to assume responsibility for the maturation of Noor Community's digital backbone. As our operations rely heavily on the Google Workspace ecosystem, this role focuses on architecting bespoke backend solutions that ensure data reliability, security, and accessibility. The Systems Architect will transform current data handling processes into a unified, automated system capable of supporting scaled operations, building the technical infrastructure that makes our pedagogical vision materially possible.

Core Responsibilities

  • Architect and maintain backend automations using Google Apps Script and JavaScript to synchronise data across Forms, Sheets, and Drive
  • Develop and implement protocols for efficient processing, storage, and visualisation of educational data
  • Construct accessible, intuitive front-end dashboards enabling non-technical staff to query databases and generate reports
  • Continuously evaluate and refine database structure to support longitudinal tracking of student attendance and assessment

Who You Are

You have a strong command of JavaScript and backend development principles. Specialised expertise in Google Apps Script and Google Cloud Platform is preferred, though general software engineering experience with capacity for rapid adaptation is valued. You can translate complex technical architectures into user-friendly operational tools, and you recognise that democratising access to data is itself a liberatory practice.

4. Student Support

The Role

This role is integral to our pedagogical commitment to holistic education. The Student Wellbeing & Support Officer ensures that our delivery models remain congruent with our trauma-informed framework, recognising that acknowledging the cognitive and emotional impact of continuous traumatic stress is a central pedagogical necessity. This position focuses on creating the conditions of safety, trustworthiness, and empowerment that make all meaningful learning possible.

Core Responsibilities

  • Analyse attendance and participation data to identify early indicators of disengagement and initiate supportive interventions
  • Design and facilitate workshops for students and staff that enhance resilience and promote mental health awareness within the academic context
  • Conduct regular audits of classroom practices and administrative interactions to ensure alignment with principles of safety and empowerment
  • Synthesise qualitative feedback from the student body to drive continuous improvement in pastoral care methodologies

Who You Are

You have an academic or professional background in psychology, counselling, social work, or student services. You possess comprehensive understanding of psychosocial support frameworks in educational settings and advanced interpersonal communication skills. You understand that the practice of psychosocial accompaniment, grounded in solidarity and care, is not ancillary to education but its very foundation.

5. Quality Assurance Officer

The Role

Operating in support of the Academic Manager, this officer is responsible for maintaining the rigour and coherence of our educational programmes. The role focuses on systematic evaluation of instructional quality and assessment reliability, ensuring that our curriculum consistently delivers on its promise of high-level linguistic acquisition while remaining pedagogically effective and culturally responsive.

Core Responsibilities

  • Audit lesson plans and teaching materials to verify alignment with the organisation's standards of critical pedagogy and academic excellence
  • Conduct statistical and qualitative analyses of assessment results to ensure reliability; identify trends requiring curricular intervention
  • Facilitate training sessions designed to standardise grading criteria and instructional delivery across the teaching cohort
  • Collaborate on refinement of curricular assets to ensure they cultivate learner autonomy, metacognitive awareness, and critical self-reflection

Who You Are

You have a background in TESOL, Applied Linguistics, or Instructional Design. You bring experience in academic quality assurance, teacher training, or assessment design, along with strong analytical skills regarding educational data and performance metrics. You are committed to an educational practice that equips learners with sophisticated linguistic and analytical tools, understanding language as a vital means for building bridges of understanding in a pluralistic world.
