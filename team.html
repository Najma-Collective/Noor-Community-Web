Our Team

Meet Our Community of Practice.

We are a global network of dedicated educators, researchers, and managers united by a common purpose. Our "horizontal" model means we are all co-learners, committed to supporting each other's growth.

Leadership Team

Areej Banat

Co-Founder & Director of Pedagogy

With over 20 years of experience in English language teaching, Areej is an expert in communicative, student-centered learning. She specializes in curriculum design for Business English, IELTS, and EAP, focusing on building practical, real-world skills.

Gabriel Ishmael Beckford

Co-Founder & Director of Strategy

Gabriel pivoted from a career in music to focus on English education, driven by a desire to build projects centered on local empowerment. He is a TESOL specialist and researcher at the University of St Andrews.
