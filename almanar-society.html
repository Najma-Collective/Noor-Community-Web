Al Manar Society

Noor Community is the English department of Al Manar Society, a pioneering organisation dedicated to empowering Palestinian communities through education, culture, and social development.

Who They Are

Al Manar Society is a community-based organisation committed to supporting Palestinian communities through comprehensive educational, cultural, and social programmes. Founded on principles of empowerment and self-determination, they work to create opportunities for Palestinians to thrive despite challenging circumstances.

As our parent organisation, Al Manar Society provides the foundation and infrastructure that makes Noor Community's English language programmes possible.

What They Do

Al Manar Society develops comprehensive programmes that empower Palestinian communities across multiple dimensions.

Educational Programmes

Al Manar Society develops and delivers educational programmes designed to empower Palestinian youth and adults with the knowledge and skills needed to pursue their goals and contribute to their communities.

Community Development

Through various social initiatives, Al Manar Society strengthens community bonds and creates supportive networks that help Palestinian families navigate challenges and access essential resources.

Cultural Preservation

They champion Palestinian culture and heritage through programmes that celebrate traditions, arts, and collective memory, ensuring that cultural identity remains strong across generations.

Advocacy & Support

Al Manar Society advocates for Palestinian rights and provides direct support to individuals and families, addressing immediate needs while working toward long-term systemic change.
