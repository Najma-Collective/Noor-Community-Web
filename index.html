Our Story

More Than a School. A Community of Practice.

Our work began in 2024, founded by two British Council teachers, Areej Banat and Gabriel Ishmael Beckford, to support Palestinian communities. Today, we are a global community where students and teachers are co-learners in a shared enterprise, committed to dismantling the traditional hierarchies that pervade international education.
