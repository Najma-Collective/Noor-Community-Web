Who We Are

Noor Community is a global nonprofit organization founded by educators who believe that access to quality English education is a fundamental pathway to opportunity for refugees and displaced learners worldwide.

We combine rigorous academic excellence with cultural responsiveness, offering tuition-free, accredited English language programmes taught by qualified volunteer educators from around the world.

Our approach is rooted in pedagogical justice—the principle that every learner deserves not just access to education, but access to world-class, transformative education that honors their identity and unlocks their full potential.

Why Noor Is Different

We're not just teaching English. We're building a global community of learners and educators committed to justice, excellence, and transformation.

🌍 Culturally Responsive

Our pedagogy honors diverse identities and experiences. We practice "rooted cosmopolitanism"— celebrating cultural roots while building global competence.

🎓 Academically Rigorous

World-class curriculum designed by experienced educators. Our standards match top universities and language institutes worldwide.

💯 Completely Free

Zero tuition. Zero hidden fees. Education is a right, not a luxury. We're donor-funded to keep it that way.

🤝 Community-Powered

Taught by 150+ qualified volunteer educators worldwide. When you learn with Noor, you join a global family committed to your success.
