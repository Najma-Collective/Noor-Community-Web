Yara Maps Soundwalks for Littles and Elders

A 15-year-old facilitator designs multisensory routes that honor everyone’s pace and stories.

When lockdowns eased, Yara noticed that her siblings and grandparents struggled to walk more than a few blocks. She created Noor's first soundwalk syllabus to help them ease back into the city.

Category
Student Voices
Published
April 21, 2024
Location
Tripoli, Lebanon
Featuring
Yara Dergham (Wellness Guide)

Every activity in this Student Voice feature is anchored to Noor's English language pathway, so learners log new vocabulary, practice academic sentence frames, and rehearse oral fluency while they build.

Designing for accessibility

Each route is mapped with icons showing shade, benches, and water. QR codes play audio prompts recorded by aunties and neighborhood bus drivers. Yara includes "pause rituals": breathing cues, poetry snippets, and stretch reminders, so that participants regulate together.

Soundwalk kits

The kits fit into a sling bag: laminated maps, tactile cards for visually impaired walkers, and a tiny speaker. Students rotate as "sound hosts" who narrate sensory details into the mic. The crew logs heart rates and emotions to track how the walks support stress regulation.

Impact

Families now book private soundwalks for birthdays or grief anniversaries. Local shopkeepers adjust delivery schedules to keep sidewalks open during the walks. Yara hopes to turn the routes into downloadable playlists so other Noor hubs can remix them.
