Quarterly Update: Funding Momentum & Student Stipends

New philanthropic commitments unlock stipends for 60 students and help us scale compliance infrastructure.

We closed Q1 with £1.2M in renewed grants plus two new multi-year gifts. Here’s how those resources are being deployed.

Category
Community News
Published
April 25, 2024
Lead contact
Lina Farah, Managing Director

This update showcases how Noor's English programmes scale: linking community infrastructure, donor support, and educator leadership to rich language learning experiences.

Highlights at a glance

  • £450k renewal from the Aurora Fund dedicated to student stipends.
  • $300k USD from the Open Field Collective to scale the Noor Learning Lab Network.
  • A £250k unrestricted pledge from community donors across the UK.

Student stipends

Sixty students across our programmes will receive monthly stipends averaging £150 to cover transportation, meals, and data bundles. Stipend disbursements will be managed through trusted community organisations to ensure compliance with local regulations.

Investing in compliance and care

We are onboarding a shared services partner to streamline safeguarding checks, financial reporting, and partner onboarding. This reduces administrative friction for our international partners and accelerates student support.

Transparency commitments

We are committed to transparency in our grant management and are publishing a stipend playbook so other grassroots programmes can adapt our process.

“This quarter’s funding is about trust,” Lina explains. “Donors see that when we invest directly in student wellbeing, academic excellence follows.”

What’s next

We are preparing for our annual audit and will release a public dashboard that visualises stipend usage, attendance, and learning milestones. Subscribe to our newsletter for updates.
