Hassan Engineers Neighborhood Scoreboards

Pickup basketball now doubles as a data literacy class thanks to an enterprising 16-year-old.

Hassan grew up keeping score on notebook paper. When he inherited a pile of broken LED billboards, he saw a new way to document streetball and literacy gains.

Category
Student Voices
Published
April 10, 2024
Location
Tunis, Tunisia
Featuring
Hassan Gharbi (Sports Tech Lead)

Every activity in this Student Voice feature is anchored to Noor's English language pathway, so learners log new vocabulary, practice academic sentence frames, and rehearse oral fluency while they build.

From e-waste to spectacle

Hassan and his crew harvested LED panels, rewired them with solar batteries, and mounted the displays to an old futsal cage. Each board shows live scores plus shout-outs to students who met their reading goals that week.

Data as community pride

Every player logs rebounds, assists, and "care stats" (like who refilled the water jugs). Hassan converts the numbers into infographics that hang at the hub. Teachers now use the stats to teach ratios and percentages.

What's next

The crew is building a "scoreboard toolkit" so other hubs can remix the project. Hassan wants to add audio cues for visually impaired fans and integrate attendance tracking for night school students.
