Bridge Weeks Turn Exam Prep Into Care

We treat the days between school exams and Noor programming as sacred transition space.

Instead of rushing back into our full timetable, we now hold "bridge weeks": lighter days focused on nervous system repair and logistics.

Category
Pedagogical Insights
Published
April 12, 2024
Authored by
Hiba Al-Rifai, Programme Director

This facilitator insight sits inside our English for Academic Purposes toolkit, offering concrete moves that keep language objectives visible while delivering hands-on content.

What bridge weeks include

  • Morning bodywork with visiting aunties and PT students.
  • Open office hours for paperwork, benefits support, and exam appeals.
  • Mini-workshops on grief, financial planning, and creativity.

Why it works

Students arrive to Noor programming already regulated, facilitators have time to assess needs, and families know when to schedule rest. Attendance dips less after exam season because the transition honors the burnout.

Tools

We built a shared calendar template, SMS reminders, and a "slow playlist" to set the tone. Partner hubs now request bridge-week coaches.
