Safiya's Solar Kitchen Bakes Math Lessons

Teen girls in Cairo transform rooftops into outdoor classrooms where parabolic cookers and algebra mix.

Safiya spent lockdown tinkering with pizza boxes and mirrors. Now she leads Noor's solar kitchen lab, inviting neighbors to eat the results.

Category
Student Voices
Published
April 14, 2024
Location
Cairo, Egypt
Featuring
Safiya El-Masry (Solar Chef)

Every activity in this Student Voice feature is anchored to Noor's English language pathway, so learners log new vocabulary, practice academic sentence frames, and rehearse oral fluency while they build.

Math through meals

Students sketch cookers on graph paper, calculate reflection angles, and budget ingredients. Each lab ends with a "math in motion" circle where participants explain the equations that got their bread baked.

Girl-led safety

Safiya designed buddy systems, shade tents, and hydration check-ins so hijabi students could work comfortably. Caregivers prep snacks, while Safiya's little brothers handle elevator duty for heavy pots.

Scaling the kitchen

The crew published an open-source zine featuring cooker templates, safety checklists, and dessert recipes. Other Noor hubs now host "sun potlucks" inspired by Safiya's notes.
