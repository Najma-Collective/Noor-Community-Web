Micro-Ateliers Make Math Feel Like Craft

We broke algebra down into 20-minute ateliers that end with tangible keepsakes.

Our Tunis hub struggled with large math blocks after Ramadan. We responded by turning equations into tiny art studios.

Category
Pedagogical Insights
Published
April 22, 2024
Authored by
Lina Mzoughi, Math Atelierist

This facilitator insight sits inside our English for Academic Purposes toolkit, offering concrete moves that keep language objectives visible while delivering hands-on content.

Structure

Each micro-atelier begins with a sensory provocation (textiles, spices, or clay) followed by a single math maneuver. Learners manipulate materials whilst narrating their process. The session ends with a photographed artifact that becomes part of a communal "math gallery."

Key ingredients

  • Constraints. Twenty minutes, two materials, one equation.
  • Roles. Students rotate between maker, documentarian, and witness.
  • Reflection. Each group records an audio postcard explaining what shifted.

Impact

Attendance stabilized, and facilitators reported fewer behavior incidents. Algebra projects now live on shelves instead of inside binders, reminding everyone that proof can be tactile.
