Pocket Sensory Cards Calm Classrooms

Portable, student-made cards cue co-regulation rituals in seconds.

Facilitators in Colombo asked for a low-cost way to honor sensory needs without pausing entire lessons. Students designed the solution.

Category
Pedagogical Insights
Published
April 18, 2024
Authored by
Nadheera Perera, Wellness Coordinator

This facilitator insight sits inside our English for Academic Purposes toolkit, offering concrete moves that keep language objectives visible while delivering hands-on content.

Card anatomy

Each card includes an icon, a grounding prompt, and a QR code that links to a 30-second audio cue. Students laminate the cards and attach them to lanyards or pencil cases.

Using the deck

When a learner flashes a “blue swirl” card, peers know to lower lights and switch to a whisper voice. A “gold sun” card signals a need for movement; facilitators rotate in a stretch or a hand massage station.

Scale

The deck now exists in Sinhala, Tamil, Arabic, and English. Families requested extra sets for home routines, and our digital team is developing a printable pack for partner schools.
