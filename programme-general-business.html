General & Business English

General & Business English: Real-World Problems, Real-World Skills.

Our courses use Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT), where you acquire language by solving tangible, real-world problems.

Our Learning Model

Step 1: Pre-Class Scaffolding

We use robust out-of-class activities, including interactive micro-lessons, to prepare you for class—even with limited bandwidth.

Step 2: Dynamic Collaboration

Classroom time is dedicated to dynamic, collaborative engagement and problem-solving with your peers.

Step 3: Integrated Writing

You will learn to master professional genres—like CVs, business plans, and persuasive emails—by deconstructing and then constructing them.

Rehearse Your Future

Our project-based modules are “worldbuilding” sessions.

Featured Project: The NGO Funding Challenge

Role: You are a Project Manager at a new, youth-led NGO in Nablus providing digital literacy training to graduates.

The Task: Your team must secure funding. You will analyze two real-world grant proposals—the “Al-Quds Innovation Fund” (fast, small, local) and the “Digital Futures Foundation” (large, slow, global).

The Outcome: You will practice using hypothetical language (the second conditional) to debate the pros and cons, and then present a formal justification for your choice to stakeholders. This single project teaches negotiation, strategic planning, and high-level justification skills.
