Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Students, Volunteers, and Supporters.

General

Our core vision is "Education as a Practice of Self-Determination." We are an international network of educators united by a shared vision of liberation through education, committed to nurturing the conditions for a future of one's own making.

Noor Community was founded in 2024 by Areej Banat and Gabriel Ishmael Beckford, two educators with extensive experience who wanted to create an empowered, community-led model of education.

This is our core pedagogical approach. It means we recognize that profound learning requires safety. We create predictable, empowering, and relationally secure spaces that focus on collective well-being and affirm identity, which allows students to take the intellectual risks that rigorous learning demands.

This is a learning outcome. We cultivate "rooted cosmopolitanism" where a strong, confident sense of local identity becomes the very basis for ethical and effective global engagement. We frame English as a language of connection, not assimilation.

Our programmes are designed for Palestinian university students, graduates, and professionals who want to build their skills for their career or higher education.

It is a 3-step process: 1) You submit an online application, 2) You are invited to a virtual placement interview, and 3) Successful applicants are onboarded to our learning platform.

Instead of high-stakes tests, we use a portfolio-based model. This allows you to curate your best work, reflect on your progress, and receive continuous feedback, fostering deeper learning and "mature learner autonomy".

You will need a stable internet connection and a device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) capable of running Zoom and accessing our Moodle-based learning platform.

For Volunteers & Supporters

Volunteers receive advanced professional development in healing-centred pedagogy, join a supportive international community of practice, and apply their skills to an ethically-grounded project.

We seek reflective, collaborative, and committed professionals. Specific roles include English Language Teachers, Curriculum Developers, and Research Specialists.

It means we are a "horizontal" learning community. Our educators are valued partners and co-learners, not just instructors. It's a professional home where you can reconnect with the craft of teaching in a purposeful context.

100% of your donation provides the material foundation for our work. It powers our digital classrooms (Zoom, Moodle), maintains our global platform, and—critically—upholds our non-negotiable principle of fair pay for our local Palestinian managers.

We are committed to transparency in all operations. We publish our financial information and fund allocation breakdowns annually. You can find this on our "Financials & Transparency" page.

We partner with value-aligned organizations. Our featured partner is ALanqa, with whom we deliver specialized soft skills training in communication, teamwork, and leadership.

Mission

Noor Community: Education and Liberation. A global network creating pathways to self-determination through rigorous, compassionate education.
