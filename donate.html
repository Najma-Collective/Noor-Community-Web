How Your Solidarity Creates Impact

We are a lean, grassroots organization. Every donation is transformed into the essential tools that make our mission possible.

  • computerPowering Our Digital Classrooms: Your gift secures the Zoom and Moodle subscriptions that function as our protected, reliable classrooms.
  • publicMaintaining Our Global Platform: Your support keeps our online presence running, connecting our community across borders.
  • diversity_2Upholding Fair Pay for Local Leadership: Your donation allows us to pay our local Palestinian managers a fair, living wage. This is a non-negotiable principle, grounding our work in mutual respect and economic justice.

By donating, you are providing the material foundation for a community built on the belief that a more just and equitable future is possible.
